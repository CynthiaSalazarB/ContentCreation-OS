# Personal Brand — Operating Page (template)

> **Setup:** copy this file to `config/personal_brand.md` and fill it in with your own brand.
> `personal_brand.md` is gitignored — your real brand definition never leaves your machine.
> This is the file the `script_angles_agent` reads verbatim as LLM context for brand-fit scoring and angle generation, so write it for an LLM: concrete, specific, one page.

---

## Spine (who you are — never changes)

**One bold sentence that captures the through-line of everything you make.**

Short forms:
- A one-clause version for bios.
- An even shorter tagline.

*(Tip: the spine should be an intersection — the 2–3 things only you combine — not a job title.)*

---

## This year's focus (the active goal)

**The ONE outcome your content serves right now** (get hired, launch a product, grow an audience…).

Everything else you someday want is real but **parked** elsewhere. Not on the daily plate.

### Depth vertical (the ONE thing you go deep on now)

Name your niche and its layers, e.g.:

- **Anchor (love it):** your home stack/craft
- **Depth (in demand):** the marketable skill you're deepening
- **Just enough:** supporting skills you keep minimal
- **Ship:** how your work reaches the world

---

## Lanes (where every idea goes — filters, not folders)

| Lane | Purpose | Weight (now) |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **Lane 1** — e.g. technical authority | Why this lane exists | ~50% |
| **Lane 2** — e.g. creative differentiation | … | ~25% |
| **Lane 3** — e.g. reflection/connection | … | ~25% |

*(The angles agent maps every idea to one of these lanes.)*

---

## The Filter (questions before you make/post anything)

1. **Spine** — does it show what you stand for?
2. **Goal** — does it advance this year's focus?
3. **Bridge** — if it's off-topic, can it be tied back to your core themes?

All yes → make it. Otherwise → it's a journal entry, not a post.

---

## Audience (the one person on the other side)

Describe a single reader/viewer: what they're building, what they struggle with, what they want to become, why your mix speaks to them.

---

## Voice

- 3–5 bullets describing tone (e.g. honest but concise; concept-first; curious framing; serious about the work, light about yourself).

---

## Signature frames (reusable)

- Named, repeatable content patterns of yours (e.g. "life topic → technical twin"). The angles agent will reuse these by name.

---

## Non-negotiables / guardrails

- Things you never post, framings you always avoid, rules that keep you on-brand.
