# ContentCreation-OS — Vision (Source of Truth)

> The stable "what & why." Changes rarely. For *how it's built* see [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md); for *what's coming* see [WHATS-NEXT.md](WHATS-NEXT.md).

---

## One-liner

A local-first, modular "second brain + brand co-pilot" that helps me stay aware of the world, capture ideas, and turn the right ones into on-brand content — **AI curates, I decide.**

---

## The deeper why

I keep things that seem separate and make them whole. ContentCreation-OS is that philosophy as software: independent modules integrated into one coherent system — the same way I'm integrating my logical and creative selves into one person, in public.

So this project is three things at once:
1. A **real tool** I use daily.
2. A **portfolio piece** that proves how I think (systems + architecture).
3. A **mirror** of my brand spine: integration.

---

## Who it's for

- **Primarily me** — optimize for simplicity and daily use.
- Designed cleanly enough that it *could* work for others: swap in your own brand config (`config/personal_brand.example.md`) and feeds, and the same engine runs on your voice instead of mine.

---

## What it does

- **Awareness** — a daily dashboard of AI + world news (a living feed, not an archive).
- **Content radar** — scans news/ideas against a brand definition and flags what's worth a video, with angles.
- **Capture** — quick inbox for raw thoughts from anywhere (CLI or Telegram).
- **Curate** — AI scores/suggests; the human approves, rejects, or reframes.
- **Connect** — modules feed each other (life → content), orchestrated into one flow.

---

## Core principles

- **Human-in-the-loop** — AI suggests; I make the final call.
- **Local-first** — my data stays on my machine; Notion is the dashboard.
- **Modular** — independent modules connected via shared storage.
- **Progressive** — JSON before SQLite, rules before LLM, one module at a time.
- **Integration over fragmentation** — every module earns its place by connecting, not sprawling.

---

## Non-goals (what this is NOT)

- **Not a news archive** — news is disposable daily awareness, not a filing cabinet.
- **Not fully autonomous** — no agent acts without my approval on what matters.
- **Not over-engineered** — no Docker, no FireCrawl, no infra I don't need yet.
- **Not a rebrand machine** — new interests get *placed*, not chased.

---

## Success criteria

- I read my world+AI dashboard each morning without thinking about it.
- When something is video-worthy, the system tells me *and* gives me an angle.
- My content feels like **one voice**, not fragments.
- The system itself becomes a portfolio piece that attracts opportunities.
